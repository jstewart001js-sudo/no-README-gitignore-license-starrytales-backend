1. Overview

StarryTales ("we," "us," "our") is a subscription service that emails parents a personalized, AI-generated bedtime story featuring their child each night. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using StarryTales (the "Service"), you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, password (or other authentication method), and billing status.

Child Profile Data. To personalize your child's stories, we collect your child's first name or nickname, age range, and story preferences (e.g., favorite themes or story types) that you provide. We do not collect your child's full legal name, date of birth, school information, or photos.

Story Content. We store the prompts and preferences you submit, along with the AI-generated story text created for your child.

Delivery Data. We use the email address you provide to send nightly stories, and we may keep basic delivery/open logs to make sure stories are being received.

Usage Data. We collect basic analytics about how the Service is used — such as pages visited, sign-up funnel events, and error logs — to help us maintain and improve the Service. We do not use third-party ad-tracking tools.

Payment Information. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe, our payment processor. StarryTales does not collect or store your full card number — Stripe handles that securely on our behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate and personalize your child's nightly story using AI.
  • Deliver stories by email on your scheduled delivery time.
  • Manage your subscription, billing, and account access.
  • Send transactional emails (confirmations, receipts, delivery notices).
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

We use Anthropic (Claude) to generate story text, and Resend to deliver story emails. The child details and story preferences you submit are sent to Anthropic solely to generate that night's story, and to Resend solely to deliver it to you. We do not permit these providers to use your data to train their own models, beyond what is inherent to standard API processing under their terms.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

StarryTales accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian — not by the child directly. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13; all child profile information is supplied by the parent or guardian who creates the account.

If you believe your child's information was collected improperly, please contact us at Starrytales101@gmail.com, and we will investigate and delete it promptly.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Render and is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We retain your account and child profile data for as long as your account remains active, and we delete it upon account or story deletion, except where we're required to retain it for legal or accounting purposes.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Service — Anthropic (AI story generation), Resend (email delivery), Render (hosting), and Stripe (payment processing) — under agreements that limit their use of your data to providing services to us.
  • When required by law, subpoena, or other legal process.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, in which case your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of non-essential communications from us. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Starrytales101@gmail.com.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), gives you additional rights over the personal information we collect about you and your child:

  • Right to Know. You can request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we've collected, the sources it came from, the purposes for collecting it, and the categories of third parties we've shared it with.
  • Right to Delete. You can request that we delete personal information we've collected from you or your child, subject to certain legal exceptions (e.g., completing a transaction or complying with a legal obligation).
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing. As stated in Section 7, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of today. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism before doing so.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination. We will not deny you goods or services, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of service because you exercised any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Starrytales101@gmail.com. We will take reasonable steps to verify your identity (and, where the request involves a child's information, your status as the parent or guardian on the account) before responding.

10. Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and basic analytics to understand how the Service is used. We do not use third-party ad-tracking cookies or sell data to advertisers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or through an in-app notice. The "Effective Date" at the top of this policy reflects the most recent version.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at Starrytales101@gmail.com.
